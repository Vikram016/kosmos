It is now more than 60 years after Yuri Gagarin's flight above Earth. Humanity should have become a unified utopia of milk, honey and harmony spread throughout the Solar System... from a habitat on Mercury to exploration ships beyond Pluto... done as easily as getting onto a bus and go live in a house in a manner removed of feudalism and tax of any kind.

But this techno-social evolution has not happened because of unconsidered simple approaches.

We, Kosmos Society, are a non-profit organization founded to change the above situation by designing all necessary techno-social systems which are simplified, natural, aesthetic, cheaply built anywhere, cheaply operated, safer, reliable, easily repaired, democratic, truly revolutionary.
